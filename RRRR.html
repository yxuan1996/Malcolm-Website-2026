Really Rich, Really Robin

Magic. Mischief. Big ideas.

Join Robin on three unforgettable adventures!

Magical Mayhem

When Robin discovers a magic wand, his world becomes topsy-turvy. From monster trees to flying carpets, the magic takes Robin's on the greatest adventure. Craving for magic? Then you're in for some mayhem!

Lost in Space

Robin and his loved ones shoot off to space for an unforgettable space-caytion. Space is a thrilling world, where the moon is just a 'moonwalk' away. But when Robin becomes separated from everyone, he's lost! Are there other life forms out there? Well, Robin is about to find out!

The Spirit of Mount Hantu

There's nothing that Robin looks forward to than glamping at Mount Hantu. Glamping is a dream come true, where luxurious beds and your own personal TV await. But who is making those eerie noises at night? Robin will soon discover that Mount Hantu is not what it seems …
